# UNTRUE STEP

Ghost garage without a grid. Every hit is an event with a time in milliseconds, a spread and a drag; SELECT bends
the timing from push to swing, Loose smears it, and every so often the drums leave and come back changed.

- **Install** version {{VERSION}} from the flasher page (Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB).
- **Try it** in the browser first.
- **Manual**: everything the panel does.
- **Download** {{PKG}} for the command-line installer.
