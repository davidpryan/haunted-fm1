# HALF-LIGHT

Harmonic weather. No grid, no steps: time is in seconds. Eight voices hold a chord and move by voice-leading under a
pressure that builds for minutes and breaks in a front. SELECT is the pressure; the white keys pin drones, the black
keys ring bells; REC freezes the sky.
