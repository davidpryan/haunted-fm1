# VOICEMAIL

The ghost-vocal fragment machine. A formant singer sings phrases, not steps: a call, an answer, a chorus, a silence,
round and round over a slow pad. SELECT drops the pitch with the throat held, and Fragment, Stutter and Reverse cut
the words to pieces.
