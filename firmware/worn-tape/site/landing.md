# WORN TAPE

The loop that decays. Play a short loop; the machine wears it out, generation by generation, and grows something new
in the holes. SELECT is the tape's age, and it runs both ways.

- **Install** version {{VERSION}} from the flasher page (Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB).
- **Try it** in the browser first.
- **Manual**: everything the panel does.
- **Download** {{PKG}} for the command-line installer.
