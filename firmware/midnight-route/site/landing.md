# MIDNIGHT ROUTE

The arrangement engine. The song has a shape before it has notes: pick a route shape, set its length, tag its sixteen
stations, and the machine fills in the journey with a sub, a pad, a ghost voice and rain, each as dense as the route
says. SELECT scrubs the form.
